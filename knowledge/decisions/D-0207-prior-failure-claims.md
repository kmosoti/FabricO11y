# D-0207: Prior-failure claims

Keep an attempt observation and a separately recorded failure claim bound to
the frozen basis the claimant actually saw. Matching selected typed observations
can be evidence-referenced but are not independently verified. Missing, omitted,
unknown and conflicting inputs remain distinct. Receipt pointers and free text
are untrusted; no enforcement, content execution, or custody claim follows.
Later evidence requires a new basis and a new claim rather than changing history.
