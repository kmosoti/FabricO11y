name: multidimensional-reasoning
description: Explore difficult architecture, novel algorithms, causal debugging and uncertain tradeoffs. Skip routine mechanical work.
---
Ground the objective, constraints and observed evidence. Distinguish facts,
assumptions, deductions and predictions. Consider genuinely different alternatives
and the strongest counterexample to each. Use only dimensions that can change
the decision: representation, failure, resources, time, environment or migration.
Generator, adversary, verifier and synthesizer are perspectives, not compulsory
subagents. Choose the smallest observation that can falsify the leading idea.
Stop when further exploration has little decision value.

Return a compact decision artifact: objective, constraints, evidence, assumptions,
alternatives, falsifying observation and next discriminating check. Routine work
should bypass this process. Do not request or expose private reasoning transcripts.

This skill proposes reasoning and experiments. LogicGate manages supported
requirements/proposals and evidence applicability. Actual compilers, tests,
proof checkers and measurements establish results. Do not label a model review
as a proof or turn permission to investigate into permission to publish.
