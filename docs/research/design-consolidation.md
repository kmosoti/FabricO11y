# Design consolidation: what the experiments decide

Status: **exploratory research**, tier 6 of the [source-of-truth order](../README.md#source-of-truth). This page collapses the prototypes, measurements and hypotheses of the ledger into one practical design for Fabric, with each decision tied to the evidence behind it and the gate it still needs. It changes nothing by itself: every item below is a candidate for an ADR, a milestone or a contract change under the rules of [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), and the gates column says which. Where the evidence is against something, that is written down too.

## The design, in one paragraph

Keep the product's spine: one custody record per Batch, per-kind projections sorted by one total key under one Segment lifecycle, answers that say what they covered. Change four things, in this order. First, make every query cost its answer rather than its window, by promoting the tail key index and the threshold walk together with a file-order fallback, under the executable specification as the correctness reference. Second, keep the unsealed tail short and cheap: implement the bounded sealer as accepted, after checking its ceiling on real text, and treat the tail's entry count, not its bytes, as the quantity to bound. Third, store the payload once: a canonical record the node emits, so that the custody copy and the queryable copy are one object; adopt it on the wire only after the block tail is shown to remove the per-entry decode. Fourth, measure storage on real text from now on, because the synthetic workload hides every layout question.

## What the evidence is against

- **A query engine, planner or IR.** Every measured cost was removed by three mechanisms on the existing path; nothing in the random sets needed an operator the path lacks ([direction review](query-engine-direction.md)).
- **Template or CLP-style log compression now.** Block Zstd finds what the crude template split finds (0 to 15 % over the plain column); the known gains are in searching compressed archives at scales Fabric does not retain.
- **Node-first or series-first sort.** Nothing on logs, a few percent on metrics, and it would break the disjoint row-group property the walk and the pruning rely on.
- **A wide-event single table.** Metrics are 3 to 13 % of a Segment and want their own encodings; every surveyed system that tried kept them apart.
- **Treating any program as the oracle.** The stock differential proves sameness, not correctness; the Python oracle shares its author's reading of the contract. The specification is the reference, and the two programs are checks on different things.

## The order of work

1. D9's model-based test and D1's fallback (A4), together: a week, and the walk can then be promoted under Q2 with the soak.
2. D6 of the suite (the sealer's ceiling on real text), half a day, then D3's implementation as the milestone.
3. B3, A1, B1: the cheap storage facts, a week in all.
4. A3 and the suite's D3: the block tail and projection-free Segments; the wire decision (D5) rests on them.
5. B5 and C1 at product-size Segments, then the registered gates on the target host.
