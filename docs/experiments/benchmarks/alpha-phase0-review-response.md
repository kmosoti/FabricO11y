## Invariant and executed controls

## Scope and remaining gates

The runner is a watchdog for trusted registered commands, **not** a hard filesystem quota or arbitrary-code sandbox. Polling cannot detect an arbitrary transient write/delete between samples or writes outside configured paths. Our source generator preflights and enforces its own byte cap before each write. Sampled tagged-process RSS is not an exact peak or a guarantee about a process that deliberately scrubs the token. The offline workload creates offers and an arithmetic CSV, with no committed sink; zero growing backlog is a separate fleet delivery gate and cannot be inferred from this source fixture. The FOL2 legacy baseline uses its independent exact write/reopen verification rather than the alpha offer rate contract.
